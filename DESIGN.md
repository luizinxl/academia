## Brand & Style

This design system blends **High-Contrast Bold** with **Contemporary Editorial Warmth**, engineered specifically for Havoc Fit in Americanópolis, São Paulo. It fuses raw, athletic intensity with an inclusive, boutique personal training ethos. The interface abandons sterile, clinical gym tropes in favor of an energetic, grounded visual narrative driven by Brazilian warmth, body autonomy, and urban community strength.

The aesthetic balances punchy, condensed headline typography against warm organic surfaces, punctuated by deep matte charcoal architecture and electric terracotta energy accents. The voice is unapologetic, welcoming, and vibrant: inspiring members to push limits without feeling intimidating, upholding an explicitly LGBTQ+-affirming and community-first culture.

## Colors

The color palette establishes high visual tension through warm contrast, optimized for a dark color mode environment:

- **Primary (`#D95D37`) — Terracotta Flame:** The kinetic pulse of the studio. Used for high-priority calls to action (e.g., "Agendar Aula", "Matrícula"), live session indicators, badges, and active state highlights.
- **Secondary (`#262525`) — Matte Charcoal:** Structural, grounding, and deliberate. Functions as dark container blocks, navigation rails, and punchy athletic display cards.
- **Tertiary (`#E87C5A`) — Warm Ember:** A vibrant step-up tint for interactive hover states, secondary highlights, and micro-metric graphs.
- **Neutral (`#FDF8EA`) — Crema Paulista:** The canvas foundation adapted for dark mode surfaces, providing a tactile, cream undertone that feels premium, human, and sun-warmed.

### Semantic Tones & Accents
- **Surface Dark:** `#1D1C1C` for dark-mode cards and hero split-panels.
- **Surface Muted:** `#F4EDD6` for card surfaces on top of Crema Paulista.
- **Text On Primary:** `#FFFFFF` on Terracotta.
- **Text Muted:** `#736E65` for metadata, captions, and secondary label details.

## Typography

Typography establishes an authoritative rhythm using two complementary typefaces:

1. **Display & Headings (Anton):** Condensed, heavy, and athletic. Derived from classic industrial display typography (comparable to Impact, but tuned for modern digital rendering). Rendered in uppercase for section heroes, dynamic banners, and motivational triggers (e.g. `TREINO PERSONALIZADO`, `FORÇA & RESPEITO`).
2. **Body & Utility (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Modern grotesque sans with open counters and balanced curves. Provides effortless readability across Brazilian Portuguese diacritics (`ã`, `õ`, `ç`, `ê`), maintaining clarity on schedule grids, booking flows, and instructional cards.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model combines an asymmetric fluid grid with distinct color blocking:

- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout with 24px gutters and 48px canvas margin. Split-screen panels (e.g., Crema on the left 7 columns, deep charcoal on the right 5 columns) provide theatrical weight for studio photography and live workout statistics.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with 20px gutters and 32px margins. Side-by-side elements collapse to 4/4 or stacked 8-column hero banners.
- **Mobile (<768px):** 4-column layout with 16px gutters and 20px outer margin. Asymmetric card badges and floating pill CTAs snap to full-width thumbsafe bottom anchors.

Section spacing uses rhythmic multiples of 8px. Spacing between high-energy content groups uses `space-xl` (40px) or double (`space-xl` * 2) to maintain breathing room around dense, bold headlines.

## Elevation & Depth

This design system avoids generic, fuzzy drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Layering** and **Crisp High-Contrast Offset Blocks**:

1. **Asymmetric Floating Badges:** Floating metric cards (e.g., "20+ Treinadores", "Nível Avançado") use a signature hard offset shadow: `4px 4px 0px #262525` on light surfaces or `4px 4px 0px #D95D37` on dark surfaces.
2. **Surface Layering:** 
   - Level 0 (Base Canvas): Crema Paulista (`#FDF8EA`)
   - Level 1 (Quiet Cards): `#F4EDD6` with no border
   - Level 2 (High-Impact Hero Pods): Deep Charcoal (`#262525`) with Terracotta details
   - Level 3 (Action Overlays & Popovers): Crisp 1.5px border in `#262525` with an ambient glow (`0px 8px 24px rgba(217, 93, 55, 0.15)`)

## Shapes

The shape system employs roundedness level **2**:
- Small UI controls, pills, inputs, and chips utilize consistent `0.5rem` (8px) corners.
- Interactive cards, studio schedule tiles, and floating statistic modules use `1rem` (16px) corners.
- Large focal graphic containers (such as the terracotta photo framing shape from the visual reference) scale up to `1.5rem` (24px) to soften the aggressive energy of the condensed typography.
- Status badges and tag chips utilize `rounded-full` (9999px) for friendly, humanistic pill contours.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (CTA):** Terracotta (`#D95D37`) background, text in white (`#FFFFFF`), uppercase Anton or bold Plus Jakarta Sans (`label-lg`), `0.5rem` radius, padding `12px 24px`. Micro-hover: shift up 2px with active background `#E87C5A`.
- **Secondary Action:** Charcoal (`#262525`) surface, white label, subtle terracotta edge accent on focus.
- **Ghost / Outlined:** Transparent surface, 1.5px solid Charcoal border, charcoal text; hover shifts to solid `#F4EDD6`.

### Cards & Studio Pods
- **Metric Floating Card:** Charcoal `#262525` background, white label, vibrant terracotta data point (`Anton` 32px), subtle sparkline graphics.
- **Coach / Class Tile:** Cream container (`#F4EDD6`) with a 16px border-radius, featuring high-contrast trainer photography, inclusive pronoun chips, and class tags (e.g., "HIIT", "Mobilidade", "Força").

### Form Fields & Inputs
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF` or `#F4EDD6`, 1.5px border in `#262525` (unfocused: 20% opacity, focused: 100% opacity in `#D95D37`), inner padding `14px 16px`, label in `label-md`.

### Chips & Badges
- **Status / Category Chips:** Pill-shaped, background `rgba(217, 93, 55, 0.12)`, text `#D95D37`, `6px 14px` padding.
- **Community Tag:** Pill-shaped charcoal background `#262525`, text `#FDF8EA`, small leading dot or pride heart glyph.

### Class Schedule Grid (Domain-Specific)
- Interactive time-block table showing weekly availability, trainer avatar, capacity progress bar, and instant "Agendar" button, adhering to high contrast accessibility guidelines.
